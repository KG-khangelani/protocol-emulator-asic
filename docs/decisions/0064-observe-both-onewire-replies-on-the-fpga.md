# D64 — Exercise the seventeenth physical 1-Wire slot with a programmable peer

25 November 2026. E0061 established physical error handling and modeled positive replies. Use its same 85-word image and FPGA bitstream on idle FabricFox board 4. A second state machine on the RP2350 will observe DQ on GPIO19, sink the line for presence, sample all sixteen command bits, and compare its decoded word against the two command bytes before enabling a reply. Drive only low or release. A separate sampler will record both connected inputs, the FPGA drive command and HALT. The peer and sampler share the RP2350 oscillator. [E0061 controls and program](../../evidence/E0061-onewire-board-controls/README.md)

Run four combinations of nominal 25 or 55 µs presence-delay settings and reply zero or one. Require a reset of 500 µs and 17 command/read slots. Allow one 200 ns sample of uncertainty on the 2 µs, 62 µs and 76 µs controller timings. Verify physical reset and presence polarity, a presence start 15–60 µs after reset release, a presence width of 60–240 µs, and the wire value at 15 µs in the last slot. Require both the peer and an offline decoder to recover bytes `CC B4` least-significant bit first. Require matching firmware markers for both replies and a released line at the end.

Use two further controls with the peer configured to expect `CD B4` and to send zero only on a match. Predict that it will record `CC B4`, reject the command and leave the read slot pulled high. The firmware can report a plausible one in that situation. A valid peer result therefore also requires the command-acceptance record. Preserve rejected trials and raw traces. This is a physical programmable-peer trial. The sensor-specific measurement still needs separately identified externally powered and parasite-powered DS18B20s.
