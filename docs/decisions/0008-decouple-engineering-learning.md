# D8 - Progress engineering independently from owner fluency

Date: 2026-10-02. Status: adopted by explicit owner approval.

Need: M0 technical closure is complete, while the owner-fluency teach-back is
pending. Treating that personal learning checkpoint as a stop-work gate would
prevent simulation, implementation, and verification progress during periods
when the owner has limited review capacity.

Decision: engineering milestones may progress independently after their
technical prerequisites pass. Learning records remain separate and conservative:
M0 fluency stays `PENDING`, its skills stay `NOT_DEMONSTRATED`, and automation
must not infer competence from engineering results. Reports must state both the
engineering milestone and the independent learning status.

Consequences: M1 implementation can proceed now, but neither M0 fluency nor M1
fluency is satisfied by this work. Hardware spending, competition submission,
and fabricated-silicon claims remain separately reserved decisions.
