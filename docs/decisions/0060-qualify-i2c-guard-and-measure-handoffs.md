# D60 — Verify the I²C guard and pursue receiver-end measurements

25 November 2026. Start with the released 96-word combined I²C transaction and the council's 108-word candidate. The candidate inserts a 24-clock wait after each of twelve SCL-low instructions and removes the same time from the low dwell. Use the release assembler, the powered E7/67/00 handoff, and the E0047 submitted netlist and functional SRAM. Keep the digital timebase at 50 MHz and the bus observation grid at 5 ns. [Historical contract](../../evidence/E0049-review-council/panel-b/contracts/retime-contract.md) · [Receiver-end criteria](../research/council-2026-11-24/next-experiments.md)

Run the same 156 schedules on both images: one unstretched write/read, a NACK at each of the three acknowledgment positions, and four stretch timings at each of 38 SCL releases. Compare all 159 original guard and timing-control traces to their retained hashes, including the 8-, 170- and 171-clock controls. The first two controls should pass. The 171-clock case should violate the data-valid limit even while returning the expected byte. Also exercise each image on 16 clean transactions across four addresses and four complementary payload pairs. Require full byte readback on every launch, agreement between Icarus RTL, Verilator RTL and the submitted functional netlist, the independent public-pin oracle's expected result, and the specified cleanup deadline for every timeout. Measure actual timing and retain the first unexpected trace. Record any case where the two images differ in transfer duration.

For SPI, identify the actual controller, clock, selection gap, next bus use and load. For UART, keep the sender isolated through readback and confirm release before its first start. Use calibrated driven-high, driven-low and released controls on the target branch. For I²C, measure receiver threshold crossing order on a declared load before selecting firmware. The optional DS18B20 trial requires both supply configurations and an independent count of 17 slots with the correct power response. Check available instruments and public lab wiring. Keep FPGA digital, gate simulation, calibration and real-sensor results separately attributable. [SPI bench procedure](../../evidence/E0057-remote-spi/README.md#next-electrical-test)
