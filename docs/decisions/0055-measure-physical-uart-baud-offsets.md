# D55 — Measure two-frame delivery with a faster and slower physical UART peer

25 November 2026, South Africa. The remote instrument search started at 22:03 UTC on 24 November. Two idle Welland FabricFox boards expose UIO1 through the RP2350 and PMOD HAT. The published mapping puts it on FPGA package pin 4, controller GPIO26 and header JB2. The public controls and wiring identify no calibrated current or differential-voltage channel on that branch. The LabsLand Prism4 DE1-SoC station does have a multiplexed two-channel ADALM2000. Its published configuration establishes neither a target-side sense resistor, a characterized opposing bias nor calibration at the selected pin. Its FPGA also requires a separate implementation. The SIGPT racks list UART, JTAG, on-chip debugging and a camera. These records leave BENCH-T01 open. ## Experiment fixed before the physical campaign

The council found that a faster UART transmitter reduces the time available to copy the first byte. E0052 used an exact 434 clocks per bit and a single joint waveform generator. Test whether the same routed FPGA and 32-byte receiver can deliver two bytes with the peer approximately 2% fast or slow, at several launch delays. Reserve up to one hour from 22:03 UTC for this campaign, including diagnosis and reporting. Keep bulk data in a fresh H: build directory. [Council analysis](../../evidence/E0049-review-council/panel-a/uart/declaration.md) · [Physical baseline](../../evidence/E0052-remote-uart/README.md)

- Cold-load and verify all 32 bytes for each case. Keep the public upload, idle-board and GPIO-ownership checks from D54. Use a 12.5 MHz project clock, a separately programmed transmitter and an input-only digital capture referenced to the first rising clock edge. All three state machines use one nominal 125 MHz RP2350 clock.
- Send consecutive `55 AA` frames at 4,254, 4,340 and 4,429 controller ticks per bit. Each receiver clock takes ten ticks. At each rate use three launch delays separated by four controller ticks (32 ns nominal). Add `A5 3C` at the fast rate, a bad first stop at the fast rate and a bad second stop at the slow rate. Repeat on a second idle board if one is available.
- Retain the entire pair as GPIO samples every two controller ticks (16 ns nominal). Reconstruct every wire transition from the intended frame bits independently of the transmitter code. Require idle on each side, the correct transition count and positions within one sample of the ideal offsets from the observed first falling edge. At each rate the three first-fall indices must move by two samples per delay step, within one sample. A separate counter must observe 100,000 project-clock pad periods within 2% of 8 ms including software overhead.
- Require both intended bytes and terminal `A7` on every good pair. Require `E6` with no delivered bytes on either faulty stop, retaining the tentative first byte for a second-stop fault. The observed maximum poll interval plus maximum read sequence plus 2 µs timer allowance must be strictly less than 340.32, 347.20 or 354.32 µs for the fast, nominal or slow source respectively. An unreadable waveform, missing control, service overrun or mismatched byte rejects that run. Preserve failures.
- The measured digital states and programmed offsets apply to this FPGA fixture. Independent oscillator drift, physical high impedance, absolute frequency and release time require other instrumentation. Keep all released chip and FPGA inputs fixed.
