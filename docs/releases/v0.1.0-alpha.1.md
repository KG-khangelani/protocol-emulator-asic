First research prerelease of the reloadable Protocol Emulator ASIC and its
pinned Linux amd64 verification workbench.

### Included

- Public-pin program loading, bounded input waits, loops and reusable shifting.
- Qualified digital UART transmit, bounded last-byte receive and the merged
  bounded host-service work from PR #8.
- Actual-program SPI sensitivity controls and the four-mode schedule screen.
  Under the written held-response assumption, the mode-0 and mode-2 schedules
  fit the experimental eight-word store and pass public loader/readback tests.
- The Protocol atlas, source-backed specifications and checked-in evidence.

### Evidence and limits

[E0026](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/tree/v0.1.0-alpha.1/evidence/E0026-program-sensitivity)
retains the program proof and expected failing controls.
[E0027](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/tree/v0.1.0-alpha.1/evidence/E0027-four-mode-schedule-screen)
retains 6,176 reference-model positives and 36 public RTL cases per simulator
(26 accepted and 10 expected rejections), with matching simulator records.

The tested phase-1 schedule family uses ten words and does not fit the actual
eight-word store. This is not a proof of a global minimum. Full four-mode SPI,
continuous UART receive and I2C remain unqualified. The experimental compact SPI
core has not replaced production RTL. M1 physical fit/timing, target Fmax and
silicon are not established; archived M0 physical evidence is separate. Owner
fluency remains pending.

The attached publication manifest identifies the tested container digest,
source commit and checks. See the
[publication guide](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/v0.1.0-alpha.1/docs/releases.md)
for use and future updates.
