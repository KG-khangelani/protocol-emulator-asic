This research prerelease resolves the matched 8x4 CMOS5L comparison and prepares
the next integration baseline.

- Compact10 has ten program words, 76,230.2 square micrometres of routed
  standard-cell area and 6.30955% utilization. Its declared 20 ns timing model
  reports worst setup/hold slack of +4.438054/+0.122590 ns across the frozen
  corners, with zero required routed physical violations.
- Its official real-netlist tests pass all twelve public groups and reproduce
  1,052 reference SPI records, including four expected input-hold rejections.
- All ten official prechecks pass on the exact submitted layout. D35 selects
  compact10 as the next integration baseline.
- P uses 13.1456% less routed standard-cell area and eight program words, but
  fails one fanout limit: eleven loads against eight after antenna repair.
  Its dependent precheck/netlist jobs are skipped. P is not adopted under
  these conditions; its measured advantage and failures remain available.
- Both complete chips include their real loaders/stores. The actual resolved
  configurations, timing constraints and GDS tool/action identities match.
- The original default-flow failures and bounded unsuccessful tuning trials
  remain preserved. Historical 6x4/M0 evidence is unchanged.

The workbench publication manifest identifies the separately tested tools
image. The G1 artifact manifest identifies the physical source, selected pilot
and original archive checksums. Internal official-action tags remain an observed
dependency boundary.

This is an experimental pilot. Shared-core I2C, combined production promotion,
unrestricted SPI inputs, continuous autonomous UART delivery, board electronics,
fabricated silicon and owner fluency remain unqualified. G2 is the next
engineering goal; the final chip must receive fresh combined and physical
qualification before submission.
