## What the project has demonstrated

The project has one reprogrammable, single-stream GPIO timing engine. Interface 0x22 exposes 128 × 32-bit logical instructions, two transmit bytes and one completed receive byte. A real single-port 256 × 32 SRAM supplies instruction storage. Next-PC prefetch and a word-zero shadow preserve the established edge schedule. Firmware supplies protocol framing, direction and cleanup. At G2 the complete design passed nine comparison workloads, including four protocol workloads and five primitive/reload workloads. G3 shipped a versioned assembler, listings, load/readback plans and thirteen examples. G4 published the physical candidate as **v0.1.0**. G5 added **v0.1.1**, a software release demonstrating reproducible triggered waveforms and a controlled two-clock timing fault. The owner's M0 checkpoint under G6 is pending. It requires five criteria demonstrated with support; the current learning record shows zero of five. ## Where the current design changes behavior

**A selected board needs a different loading sequence.** The organizer says deselecting the user design powers down that design and loses its state. The released macro-port plans use low-enable clocks. On the qualified logic, simply forcing enable high advanced the program early. Two clocks at reserved selector `E7`, followed by `E7 → 67 → 00` with the clock held low, resolved that digital discrepancy. The submitted G4 netlist matched all 55 original G3 cases including 15 expected rejections, and its 1,160 I²C case file matched byte for byte. Both the submitted netlist and Verilator RTL reproduced all 1,262 runtime rows of the twenty applicable G5 cases. The twenty-first case explicitly pauses with enable low. [Powered launch, original failure and case files](../../evidence/E0049-review-council/powered-netlist/README.md)

That result exposes a separate hardware integration task. On the inspected ETR predecessor, UIO1 is shared with the RP2350 and the external connector. A UART transmitter idles actively high; the loader can drive the same pin low during readback. Among 720 possible monotone orders for changing the six selector bits, 360 passed through a loader read. All 120 orders clearing bit 7 first kept the tested route released. Those are enumerated digital paths, with no probability assigned to a real board. Clock ownership, break-before-make data direction, peer isolation during readback and a bounded first start need an actual controller. A warm rearm reduced clocked host work from 802 to eight steps in a measured I²C case, but an image of equal length and different content produced the same public end markers. Use warm rearm only after exact readback and exclusive custody since then. [Carrier wiring](../../evidence/E0049-review-council/panel-b/etr-fixture.md) · [Selector enumeration and warm-rearm failures](../../evidence/E0049-review-council/panel-c/README.md)

**Correct bytes can conceal a timing failure.** For the two-frame UART receiver, an exact-rate host with poll interval H and read latency L must satisfy H + L ≤ 4,340 core clocks. With a +2% faster ideal transmitter, the derived bound under the tested framing and rearm assumptions tightens to 4,254 clocks, or 85.08 µs. Eight faster-transmitter cases duplicated the second byte at the older service boundary even though the receive waveform remained good. For SPI, a previously archived exact-edge firmware profile produced raw-valid 22 clocks (440 ns) earlier than the released held-response profiles at the cost of one instruction and a changed terminal PC. Applicability depends on the peer's response envelope and the host's success signature. [UART limits and executable analysis](../../evidence/E0049-review-council/panel-a/uart/declaration.md) · [SPI public trace comparisons](../../evidence/E0049-review-council/panel-a/replay-guide.md)

For I²C, the original combined transaction has a shortest commanded SCL-fall to SDA-fall gap of 20 ns. NXP defines ordering at receiver voltage thresholds. One deliberately slow-fall model missed the order by 113.68 ns. A compensated 24-clock guard grew the image from 96 to 108 words while keeping the 402.92 µs transaction duration; the 159-case digital screen passed its expected outcomes. No pad/load violation was measured. For 1-Wire, the 85-word DS18B20 power query initially passed all eight ideal-wire responses. A target counting transitions at its own 3.0 V threshold with a 4.7 kΩ/1 nF pull-up sometimes saw only nine of seventeen slots. A plausible HALT and power marker concealed the malformed commands. Expanding slots to 76 µs gave 32/32 successes at the two modeled loads and still rejected two 2.2 nF stress cases. These are testable operating hypotheses for a named bus. **An external requester distinguishes architectures more sharply than another periodic master.** On unchanged RTL, a 59-word mode-0 target returned `A4 → 3C` and `A5 → A7`, rejecting 254 other commands. ## The deliberation that changed the decision

Four available slots accommodated a chair and three AI reviewers. Each reviewer filed a brief representing three disciplines before reading the others. The chair exchanged objections, commissioned contrary cases and asked for independent final ballots on the same one-engineer-day scenarios. The participants then challenged those ballots. Their common project material and AI environment make agreement one council judgment. The evidence for it is the set of identifiable artifacts, primary documents, calculations and independently scored observations. The zero-margin PIO case illustrates the limit of that consensus. At a reachable phase exactly on 160 ns, a strictly positive **lower** bound on additional endpoint delay would prove a miss. A positive upper bound alone leaves the outcome open. To certify the connector deadline requires an upper bound on the *whole* interval across the claimed phases and loads. The sponsor's approximate 10 ns Tiny Tapeout pad/mux estimate provides neither quantity for the PIO experiment. Actual hardware, independent reader experience, metastability constants, energy per useful bit and a common-process route for a complete programmable rival remain unmeasured.
