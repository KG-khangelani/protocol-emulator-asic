**Strongest dissent.** A digital loader cannot determine which physically connected driver owns the line or when the remote receiver crosses its input threshold. The ready, suitably instrumented bench therefore has the higher marginal value while its equipment window is open. The competing architecture argument is strongest if the next decision actually selects the computational substrate. The current 2,130.03 μm² saving from removing the timed UART instruction amounts to 2.261% of the nominal total including SRAM. It breaks released bytecode, and its manual examples use 152/160 public load steps versus 76 each. A measured change in tile class, a binding resource constraint on this scale, or a new official signoff requirement would promote a paired physical architecture pilot. The record currently establishes none of those triggers. [Measured ablation and compatibility cost](../uart-ablation/README.md)
