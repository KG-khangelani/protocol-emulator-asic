# One-wire load calculation

Declared after reading the VM/PIO pulse schedules and DS18B20 Rev 6. This is a sensitivity calculation. It uses a 3.3 V pull-up, 4.7 kΩ, ideal zero starting voltage, and illustrative 100 pF/1 nF loads. Compare the sensor's guaranteed high thresholds of 2.2 V on local power and 3.0 V on parasite power. Include the RP2040 3.3 V input guaranteed-high threshold of 2.0 V separately. Calculate release-to-threshold time, voltage after 9 µs and the maximum C for the 9 µs interval between a write-one release at 6 µs and the earliest possible 15 µs sensor sample. Also calculate latest presence onset for a sensor detecting the reset release at each named threshold and then waiting 60 µs. Anticipated by hand calculation before writing the script, a 1 nF load can exhaust the 70 µs presence and 15 µs write-one budgets for a parasite-power receiver. Actual threshold, load, supply, charge draw and propagation require measurement. No sampled input is changed in the VM, PIO or sensor simulations.
