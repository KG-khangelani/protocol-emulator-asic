# Physical signoff, synchronizer timing and the board-work priority

At 15:40 UTC, direct analysis of the two submitted layouts corroborated the exact D46 and D47 repairs. The tightest internal hold assertion survives its specified constraints. A fresh perturbation gives a concrete margin at which reopening it would take precedence over fixture development. A repeat of the existing physical run with identical inputs would exercise neither the disputed SRAM rule authority nor the missing pad and target behavior.

## The SRAM paths

Both official G4 archives identify the same minimum-delay path at the fast corner. The bank register at `_7350_/CLK` receives the edge at 0.356804 ns and its rising Q reaches 0.486899 ns. The SRAM `A_ADDR[6]` rises at 1.069597 ns. The SRAM clock arrives at 0.387456 ns. Adding the specified uncertainty of 0.250000 ns and the macro hold arc of 0.311350 ns gives a required time of 0.948806 ns. The data arrives **0.120791 ns** later. On the longest slow-corner path, the SRAM `A_DOUT[24]` clock-to-output arc contributes 5.415475 ns. Arrival at `_7498_/D` is 14.817526 ns against 20.413412 ns required, leaving **5.595886 ns** setup. The fast data uses the −40 °C 1.32 V standard-cell library and the available −55 °C 1.32 V SRAM library. The 25 °C and 125 °C configurations use their corresponding nominal and slow libraries. Each uses the single nominal extracted SPEF. [Cross-run extraction](results/signoff-audit.json)

Fresh OpenSTA 2.7.0 read the original submitted netlist, final SDC, nominal SPEF and three fast Liberty files in the pinned physical container. It reproduced +0.120791 ns with no warnings. A deliberate increase in clock uncertainty from 0.250 to 0.375 ns produced **−0.004209 ns** on that same path. A 20 ns clock still applied in both cases. This is a sensitivity test for an additional on-die uncertainty allowance. Timing uncertainty includes multiple effects, and external oscillator jitter cannot be mapped one-to-one to local same-edge hold. A slower oscillator also leaves the same-edge hold calculation intact. The candidate continues to pass the original condition. ## Power and thermal scope

The typical-corner OpenSTA report estimates 1.722670 mW at block level, including 0.948858 mW sequential, 0.657498 mW clock and 0.039750 mW macro. The pad group is zero. The nominal static grid report finds all shapes on both nets connected, estimates 0.238 mV worst VPWR drop and 0.188 mV worst VGND drop, and specifies 1.20 V for VPWR. Its two recorded commands use `-voltage_file` without `-enable_em`. The reports supply no per-transaction current waveform, dynamic droop, current-density signoff, package thermal resistance, measured temperature or clock-frequency characterization across fabricated devices. The 125 °C Liberty coordinate is a characterized operating condition. Using the reported block power to calculate board energy or temperature would require a workload, IO activity, supply, package and thermal boundary. ## Synchronizer evidence

Each first-rank flop feeds one hold-delay cell and an AND gate before the second-rank D. The eight first-to-second placement-origin Manhattan distances are 12.96–19.14 µm. Ordinary nominal-waveform Q-to-D SDF bounds across all eight chains are 0.300–0.304 ns fast, 0.447–0.452 ns typical and 0.720–0.728 ns slow. Fresh propagated-clock OpenSTA for the actual 24 paths, with the original 0.250 ns uncertainty, gives **19.231436–19.244095 ns**, **18.982946–19.002539 ns** and **18.519300–18.550413 ns** ordinary synchronous setup slack, respectively. UIO0 yields the minimum in all three. Checkers required the actual first Q, delay output, AND output and second D on each path. These figures show that the intended two-stage design survived implementation with ample conventional transfer time. [Path components and topology](results/signoff-audit.json) · [Fresh constrained transfers](results/sta-sync.json)

For a numerical system-level metastability claim, obtain the first-stage resolution time constant τ and aperture parameter T_W for the actual flop and operating conditions. Use a consistently calibrated resolution interval S, receiving clock frequency and transition rate for *each* asynchronously consumed input. Beer et al. give λ ≈ T_W f_clock f_data exp(−S/τ) for their model. Their measurements concern a different 65 nm process. Its fitted constants cannot qualify these 130 nm flops. The application also needs a failure definition, number of active crossings, duty cycles and mission-level error budget. A controller’s missed short pulse or an incoherent multibit word may violate a protocol deadline even if both synchronizer stages behave as designed. The present data does not yield a numerical MTBF. [Beer et al., IEEE ISCAS 2011, Eq. 1 and §II](https://www.ee.technion.ac.il/~ran/papers/BeerMetastabilityMeasurementISCAS2011.pdf)

## Decision and falsifiers

1. Preserve the frozen candidate and its complete current-constraint qualification. Inspect a newly authoritative SRAM rule, revised macro library, process revision or carrier requirement on receipt. A confirmed SRAM limit above 10 nm would invalidate the twenty measured sites. A justified additional fast-corner uncertainty greater than 0.120791 ns, with other terms fixed, would exhaust the observed hold slack. Independent min-RC/OCV signoff could produce a different worst path. A failed required carrier hookup or an actual out-of-envelope power requirement also returns physical integration to first engineering priority. A fast-corner targeted replay costs seconds with the installed image and PDK. Any production edit invokes the existing requalification gates and their CI/runtime bounds.
2. The first owner task remains the 30–45 minute G6 M0 setup/prediction session. The highest current engineering value is the ETR host ownership interlock and its named peer fixture. The digital host can be improved without hardware access. For a physical result, independently quiesce or isolate peer drivers, stage clock/selector/OE transitions, and observe at least both endpoint logic levels and one current-sensitive witness. The chair’s new dual-threshold simulations show why a terminal status alone can miss errors. At the illustrative 1 nF parasite load, recovering for only 8 µs after a 62 µs low can hide subsequent slot starts from a receiver that needs 11.28 µs to recognize high. A valid-looking local-power report can accompany a malformed remote frame. Panel A accepts this limitation and reports another common-trace caveat, a one-system-clock shift in PIO SPI FIFO visibility despite matched active wires and final bytes.
3. Keep the duration-neutral 24-clock I2C data guard below these tasks. Its known digital costs are twelve words and an increase from 96 to 108 words for the combined transaction. Only an identified carrier/target with a measured or bounded failure of falling SCL/SDA ordering, and a demonstrated data-valid window for all relevant transfers, makes this change compelling. Treat a qualification replay on unchanged inputs as a reproducibility check. It cannot decide the outstanding SRAM process rule, metastability fit or receiver load.
