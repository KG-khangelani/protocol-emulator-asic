# Architecture panel vote for the 16:32 UTC adjudication

Submitted 2026-11-24 16:32:20 UTC. This ballot follows a review of the current main report and raw comparison records, before reading the other panels’ final ballots.

**Keep the qualified 0x22 production baseline. Give the next delivery day to the executable powered-host path and an observed, first-time operator run. Give the next architecture day to a matched, externally clocked SPI target with abort and reselection.** A board already available with verified isolation and an independent observer moves an actual pin/clock measurement forward. The distinction matters when the team has only one engineer-day. For the competition handoff I favor the delivery task. For a decision about changing silicon I dissent from spending that day on another replay of an already matched master waveform.

| One available engineer-day | Allocation and observation that would change my choice |
|---|---|
| Delivery, no bench | Spend four hours connecting the existing offline assembler/readback result to an executable powered-sequence adapter and automated digital smoke. Spend the rest observing a fresh operator choose a release, assemble an example, explain the ownership transition and recognize good/bad terminal status. If an unassisted owner already completes and explains that exact workflow from the submitted packet, move the day to the matched target. |
| Scarce suitable bench, instruments and operator available | Use the exclusive slot for a measured loader/peer handoff. Independently capture power/select, low/stable clock, selector transitions, both OE controls and pad voltage/current for opposing and same-level turnarounds. Record actual hardware, external isolation and uncertainty. If the available fixture only exposes resolved logic and cannot detect same-level contention, redirect the slot to a measurable two-ended rise-time/receiver phase test or return to digital delivery. A predecessor fixture establishes its own behavior. |
| Named target requiring active-abort release and immediate reuse | Spend two hours on common stimuli and scoring, four on bounded optimized VM/PIO candidates, and two on the adverse states and record. Require a declared operand/configuration envelope, two full decoded replies, all rejections, watchdog and opposite-command restart. Accept a physical time guarantee only when worst measured release plus uncertainty satisfies the named limit. A requester that permits per-device isolation and a longer measured qualified rearm can favor a slower simpler architecture. |

The strongest counterargument is that losing eight of seventeen 1-Wire edges at a modeled remote threshold, or two opposing drivers asserting the same level, can escape all of the digital architecture comparisons. An available named bus and a calibrated probe that sees actual remote threshold and high impedance would move electrical characterization ahead of architecture; a known application demanding 160 ns release during either stopped clock level would instead keep the matched target first. **Early stop:** after the first independently reproduced high-stall deadline miss with no CS-sensitive state transition before the deadline, reject that specific unmodified target schedule and preserve its trace. Further full-corpus reruns of the same settings cannot rescue this conjunctive application requirement.

**Requested correction to the current report.** The sentence beginning “Across 288 RTL and eight selected submitted-netlist tests, an aborted reply” fuses the size of the whole corpus with the observation in one named abort. I would write “The suite ran 288 RTL cases and eight selected submitted-netlist repeats. In the named 2 MHz, 7 ns phase A5 abort, both the RTL and submitted-netlist traces showed MISO release 1,563 ns after CS rose while SCK stayed high, exceeding the 160 ns limit by 1,403 ns.” That assigns the latency to the actual case.

The research seat sees an existence result on the qualified ASIC and credible counterexamples to architectural exclusivity. The 85-word VM and 32-word PIO power-query programs drove the same ideal DQ schedule. The mandatory SPI host now also has one fully assembled 30-word PIO image covering four modes and two orders, exercised on one persistent SM. At startup the PIO already drives CS/SCK while the VM releases them. Across the eight directly paired TX96/RX69 cases, resolved pins and ownership agree from execution edge 1 through terminal. At the final raw-sample point S the synchronized PIO FIFO becomes nonempty at S+3, while the older exact VM firmware exposes its nondestructive raw byte at S+2. A separate phase-specific PIO binary obtains S+2 by bypassing input synchronization under a qualified synchronous-input premise. Same-PDK area, installed storage, target-end voltage, host ABI and physical completion remain separate axes. [SPI host evidence](pio-spi/README.md) · [Earlier workload and architecture debate](challenge.md)

The architecture seat sees a sharp next comparison. C's full two-opcode 59-word VM failed its named high-SCK abort at 1,563 ns. The partial, 19-word PIO one-bit responder also failed when SCK stayed high, releasing only at 700/718 ns when the fixture eventually supplied a falling edge. Its WAIT can remain stalled indefinitely if SCK does. In 480 stopped-low three-frame scenarios, the same PIO program produced the correct opposite/new bit without host work and released both times in 100–160 ns. Four after-sample tie cases exactly spent all 160 ns, leaving no numerical allowance for a physical pad. A common single-day trial should use A4/A5 and all 254 bad opcodes, 0.5–2 MHz plus irregular edges, arbitrary low/high/turnaround abort phases, opposite-command reselection, an actual watchdog and independently scored MISO impedance. Charge any second state machine, gate, package pin, register/FIFO task or host deadline. Require independently observed setup, hold, prompt release and correct new state. A capped failed program is still an outcome worth retaining. [PIO target evidence](pio-target/README.md) · [Externally clocked VM target and timeout sweep](../panel-c/spi-target)

The mathematics seat rejects an unconstrained minimum claim. A terminal-only receiver storing two arbitrary validated UART bytes needs eight more distinguishable bits for the first byte or timely external observation. The current byte-readable arbitrary 128-word program store requires at least 4,096 variable bits of lossless state. The S+3 input-availability bound applies only to the declared synchronized PIO observation point. None of these results names an optimal instruction set. The native SSP's preloaded TX FIFO alone also cannot select the correct first response bit for both A4 and A5, whose command histories differ only on their final bit. A command-dependent decode or service path has to participate. [RP2040 SSP register and FIFO data paths, §§4.4.2.4–6](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf#page=480)

The burst ablation gives a real nominal mapped trade. The paired reports contain 353 instances of the same sequential cell type and 17,293.0464 µm² sequential area. The measured 2,130.0300 µm² decrease is 7.7668% of the reference combinational cell area, 4.7633% of all logic and 2.2610% of cell-plus-macro area. The manual software uses 17/18 words and 152/160 loading steps for TX/RX, with changed terminal PCs and host predicates. The original burst encodings now fault. I would solicit a new route only for an actual fit, power or timing requirement that could plausibly be crossed by that reduction, a hardware-version/compatibility choice, and a complete workload gate. The previously acquired +0.120791 ns hold slack and reported 10 nm effective macro geometry remain reasons to preserve the current route while those conditions are absent. [Whole-top mapping and firmware limits](../uart-ablation)

The per-image dictionary merits further work only for a binding distribution or transport constraint. The 15 released files total 1,324→922 bytes, but the three I²C images alone provide 436 bytes of saving. Removing that family gives 512→546 across the other twelve. The held-out 85-word query and 59-word target compress to 126 and 109 bytes while their unchanged verified load still costs 712 and 498 steps. Selecting a tagged raw fallback for the matched 1-Wire PIO gives 72 transport bytes including its FIFO preload. This turns a native 340/68 comparison into a transport 126/72 comparison. A direct index-to-literal fetch in the single-port SRAM can add dependent reads, and a corpus saving leaves the fixed 256×32 macro's footprint unchanged. [Held-out source and measured counts](../storage-screen/held-out/assessment.md)

Continue the additive exact SPI firmware and real host-service measurements when a named target or operator would use them. Defer a larger SRAM, multi-protocol concurrency, unconditional I²C delay insertion and a scaled UART queue until one documented use case misses an agreed observable requirement. The three perspectives in this vote share one AI author, one repository, and several common judges. Their agreement provides a rationale for a ranked plan; independent silicon, a real operator and a defensible physical fixture could change it.
