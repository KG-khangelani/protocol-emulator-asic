# Research, architecture and mathematics challenge round

The panel read the independent hardware/electrical and software/design/industrial reviews after submitting its own. The debate changed the ranking of host work and produced two executable implementation alternatives for a single new transaction. Each architecture used the same declared one-target DS18B20 power-query operation. Their hardware interfaces, silicon, clock models and available peripherals differ. [Manufacturer's data sheet, Rev 6, pages 10–16](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ds18b20.pdf#page=10)

## A public host path

**Question to the software seat.** Which work makes the programmer recipe credible, and which work depends on a known board and peer? The historical assembler asks for selected-but-disabled clocks. Both independent host screens found that a direct substitution of powered enable advances the PC into execution. A distinct host recipe using two ignored E7 writes can hold the engine at its entry while capturing the declared idle. How would the host establish pin ownership and a parked clock at that handoff, and should an unavailable board delay a matched architecture test?

**Reply and disposition.** The software seat narrowed 4–8 engineering hours to the additive digital recipe and fail-fast preflight. Its staged E7→67→00 exit holds the clock parked while changing one control group at a time. Of 720 modeled monotone bit-change orders for direct E7→00, 360 visited the loader-read condition, with 840 read dwells across those orders. The ratio characterizes the enumerated permutations. It supplies no probability distribution for physical I/O updates. An opposing peer exposed overlap as an X in the digital model. Equal-value overlap can hide ownership loss in a sampled pad. A firmware record of output-release intent cannot independently establish external Hi-Z or clock stability. The seat raised complete peer-driven two-simulator and G4 netlist qualification to 1–2 engineer-days. A timed adapter/board/UART trial is another 1–2 days if a fixture is available. The ETR or its successor, final wrapper, peer isolation and actual port timing determine the board result. We agree on prioritizing the short digital integration gate, then parallelizing the matched architecture test, technical packet and human teaching tasks according to resources. [Organizer board and loading FAQ](https://blog.janestreet.com/protocol-emulator-asic-competition/#faq) · [Prior IHP mux, pinned selection wiring](https://github.com/TinyTapeout/tt-multiplexer/blob/3e496494ee9f543ec4b84e5e71bdaee25e958ce1/rtl/tt_mux.v#L289-L319)

The software seat asked which case justifies an RX queue. A new requirement that two arbitrary contiguous bytes be recovered by a terminal-only host after both stops are validated would justify testing storage or an existing-register alternative. For a fixed valid second byte, 256 first bytes must have 256 distinguishable retained outcomes, implying at least eight more bits of information somewhere in chip or external state. The current serviced contract already supplies a host-held tentative byte. Its deadline must be bounded for the intended rate and adapter. A buffer depth, physical area and real demand for terminal-only service remain to be established.

## Two unrelated hold margins

**Question to the electrical seat.** The ideal gate trace separates SCL and SDA commands by one 20 ns clock. Which calibrated condition makes an SDA guard necessary, and what upper limit would guard time face from setup, data-valid and stretch? Does that evidence warrant reopening the separately reported positive internal SRAM hold path?

**Reply and disposition.** UM10204 Table 11 note 2 specifies bus threshold ordering. The model’s commanded edge spacing alone cannot establish it. The electrical seat's ideal closed-loop replay exercised an eight-clock and a 24-clock firmware guard. The 24-clock option used 108/128 words and, after compensating a later fixed wait, preserved all 76 SCL transitions and 402.92 µs HALT for its selected write/read case. It also passed the selected NACK and stretch matrix. In that nominal trace the duration-neutral 170-clock change reached 3.44 µs latest data change, while 171 reached 3.46 µs and failed. That is one decoded trace's bracket. Margin at other PVT, load and peer timings needs measurement. The 0p4 predecessor used 30 mA UIO pads. Its pinned pad library grids at 2–40 pF give 90-to-10% fall times of 0.352–3.255 ns fast, 0.508–4.599 ns typical and 0.831–7.252 ns slow. They weaken the case for imposing a universal guard based on a postulated 100/20 ns fall-time pair. The threshold definitions also differ. Neither the actual final pad/load nor the I²C 70-to-30% fall time follows directly from that predecessor range. [NXP UM10204 Rev. 7.0, Table 11 notes 2–3 and Fig. 38](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/user-guide/UM10204.pdf#page=44)

The agreed decision variable is the measured time of SDA entering the indeterminate range minus SCL crossing 0.3 VDD, with combined uncertainty U. A lower bound above zero and all remaining bus limits met support that load. A repeatable upper bound of zero or less requires a changed guard, driver or supported load. A sign-indeterminate interval needs a better fixture or measurement. The candidate's +0.120791 ns same-edge internal fast hold slack is a separate mapped path with a mixed-temperature macro and cell model. A new applicable model, constraints, process rev or measured failure would justify repeating that gate. The present priority is the fixture or closed-loop envelope. No experiment in this panel established electrical interoperability.

## A matched operation has three feasible implementations

| Measurement | Qualified core with new firmware | Dedicated controller | Programmable RP2040 PIO port |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Resident program used | 85 × 32 bits, 340 bytes | Hardwired schedule and 16 command bits | 32 × 16 bits, 64 bytes |
| Command payload for this implementation | Embedded in the 85-word image | Embedded in synthesized logic | One 32-bit TX FIFO word, 16 useful bits, plus one forced PULL |
| Preparation and launch used here | 712 public loader/verification edges, then run | One reset and two inhibit edges, then start-bit transition | 115 MMIO operations, then one enable write |
| After launch | Zero host operations | Zero host operations | Zero host operations |
| First DQ low at 50 MHz grid edge | 2 | 1 | 51 |
| Successful terminal edge from run start | 109,503 | 109,501 | 109,551 |
| Absent / stuck terminal edges | 28,504 / 50,003 | 28,501 / 50,001 | 28,651 / 50,101 |
| Completion | HALT status and two result pins | Dedicated ready/HALT representation and two result pins | Two result pins and extra terminal pin, followed by a self-jump |
| Directly exercised functional scope | New image on existing physical-input identity, ideal RTL | New isolated fixed RTL, ideal Icarus | Officially assembled binary, Wokwi PIO instruction decoder and a digital peer |

All three delivered the prescribed marker at all eight positive corners, sent zero command bits on absent or stuck-low cases, and exposed the incorrect command and shortened reset to the same Python judge. For each implementation all recorded DQ drive and release offsets, measured from first reset-low, agree with the corresponding VM case. The PIO harness also checked 500 more system clocks of stable terminal output. Its state machine continues to execute the terminal self-jump. The VM and fixed controller expose terminal state through different mechanisms. The PIO timing port used a 1 µs instruction tick, integer divisor 50 on an assumed 50 MHz system clock and an external two-flop input model. The Wokwi implementation executes the genuine encoded words; the harness supplied physical-time ordering from the decoder's cycle count because the library's default scheduler batches instructions. This supplies a shared, testable functional projection across implementations. [RP2040 PIO instruction and register descriptions](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf#page=306) · [Pinned Wokwi PIO decoder](https://github.com/wokwi/rp2040js/blob/cab5fae8d72f44dc300dc139fb6e600f95e8031e/src/peripherals/pio.ts)

**Objections to my result.** Adding four FIFO bytes gives 68 programmable bytes versus the VM's 340 embedded bytes for this hand-written operation, a factor of five. This count omits executable host code, control-register settings, forced instructions and architecture storage. RP2040 supplies one shared 32-word memory per PIO block, four available state machines, full-width X/Y and shift registers, counters and FIFO hardware. This program leaves no resident slots for different co-resident code in that block. The VM leaves 43 logical words in its 128-word image and uses its existing single execution engine. Its physical 256×32 SRAM, validity bits and loader are paid for in silicon regardless of resident use. The fixed design has a 15-bit timer, 4-bit bit index, state, synchronizer and result. Generic `synth -noabc` retained 34 flop primitives among 349 total generic primitives. The initial ABC run timed out. No part of this screen mapped all three complete hardware substrates in a common process. Measured fixed cost provides one feasible implementation of a relaxed specification. It does not lower-bound programmable hardware.

The PIO host actually performed 32 instruction writes, 32 address-setting forced jumps, 32 SM0_INSTR reads, a restore jump and program, pin, direction, command and clock setup. The extra corrupt-word control rejected location zero before launch. Each operation is a 32-bit APB-style register transaction in the controller model. The G3 loader counts sampled clock edges with an eight-bit external bus. Counts of 115 and 712 do not establish a latency, energy or development-effort ratio. Host code compilation and atomicity, access speed, bus architecture and external isolation remain unmatched. PIO's static output pins permit late observation of an operation result, while the target response time was fixed at 10 µs and conditional on receiving both commands. Common checker and timing-document interpretations can make every candidate accept the same mistaken premise. Separate simulator paths and deliberately rejected controls increase fault discrimination on the tested cases. They supply no proof for unmodeled presence widths, analog rise times, parasite-power delivery, multidrop, environmental drift or other protocols.

The official Pico example offers a different tradeoff. It uses 17 resident PIO instructions for bit transfers and reset; its host branches on presence, sends words via TX/RX FIFO and reads a whole byte in the default configuration. It lacks the autonomous 16-command-bit/one-read-bit terminal operation with the frozen error paths. This panel's 32-word port carries the branching, timing and terminal action into the state machine and freezes the only transaction payload before launch. That changed which comparison is defensible. [Official 17-instruction example](https://github.com/raspberrypi/pico-examples/blob/0d62f75bafc2c8120d3276c3343d1a9195e909e9/pio/onewire/onewire_library/onewire_library.pio#L23-L45) · [Pinned Pico host reset and FIFO operations](https://github.com/raspberrypi/pico-examples/blob/0d62f75bafc2c8120d3276c3343d1a9195e909e9/pio/onewire/onewire_library/onewire_library.c#L33-L57)

**Decision.** The 85-word image adds a useful externally defined transaction to the project's evidence for deterministic post-fabrication reprogramming. The task was selected after examining the ISA and uses the same open-drain/timed-wait primitives already exercised by I²C. It raises confidence in reuse across single-target applications. The matched programmable PIO port rules out an assertion of autonomous-program or resident-density advantage for the VM on this job. It makes a comparable mandatory I²C combined operation and a defined workload matrix the next architectural discriminator. The fixed alternative isolates the cost of flexibility as a future engineering question. A matched programmable core still needs common pin/host ownership, independent golden behavior, complete physical memory and loader accounting, two qualification runs and a common PDK before an area or minimum comparison.

## Two limits on opcode conclusions

The exact SPI profile reuses all eight historical E0027 binaries. It evaluates the synchronized response in time to recover a raw value present only at the nominal sample edge, while preserving driven output/OE waveforms in all 112 declared cases. It weakens the claim that SPI requires the optimized late sample on the 128-word store. It leaves the system's pad phase, synchronizer aperture, timing of RX-valid, changed full terminal PC and actual named receiver requirements material. Timing variants of one protocol do not create independent evidence for substrate minimality.

**Challenge to the chair's SHIFT_BURST ablation.** A candidate that emits identical outward UART edges and raw-byte landmarks still changes the public PC, RUN/WAIT intervals and historical host's `a7` predicate. The old host must reject it where the known-image signature differs. A new handoff must verify the image, confirm the candidate’s full terminal PC/status where needed and handle stale-valid/overwritten first bytes. At P4 the dispatch schedule has the smallest margin. Deliberately violate the first and second stop separately. Raw-valid and even plausible-looking bytes can precede framing failure; the host must deliver neither byte. Test distinct first/second TX0/TX1 data, RX dummy output, early/late service, timeout and the minimum supported period. A G3 interface ID identifies hardware capability, while each loaded program determines its own terminal PC. Firmware-only emulation can establish dispensability for a declared corpus and observation policy. The measured chip still contains the burst control logic, counters and state shared with other opcodes. The silicon cost and malformed-image fault behavior of an actual removal require an isolated RTL variant and matched verification/physical runs.
