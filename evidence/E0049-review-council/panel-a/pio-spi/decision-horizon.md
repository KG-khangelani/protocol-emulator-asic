# Which decision the next comparison could change

## Research and delivery over one engineer-day

The architecture decision is whether a substrate can meet an externally timed application that the current program misses. C measured a 59-word SPI target that returns two command-dependent responses while rejecting the other 254 opcodes. When CS rose with SCK stalled, the observed 1,563 ns turnoff exceeded the proposed 160 ns limit. C's 405 follow-up simulations found two disjoint requirements for its response-edge timeout. Bounds 48–80 supported 500 kHz at the four tested phases, while 2–9 released a still-driven MISO within 160 ns in the stated abort case. A direct literal polling implementation needed 215 words against 128 available. These results eliminate specific strategies, leaving program compression, event logic, a second engine and an external switch as open alternatives.

B tested the board-switch objection on 20 RTL and 20 submitted-netlist cases. A CS-controlled bilateral switch can reopen stale drive when the next device is selected. After an aborted A5, SCK returned low at 80 ns and a legitimate A4 followed, but all eight expected 3C bits stayed released. A unilateral CS-only buffer also drives during a normal command byte whose MISO must float. Isolating the bus or leaving the switch disarmed can prevent contention while losing the requested reply. A real part shows why chip-selection timing deserves explicit per-device requirements. Microchip's 25LC256 Table 1-2 gives an 80 ns maximum output-disable delay in its 2.5–4.5 V range, identified as periodically sampled. This supplies a concrete commercial comparison point. The council's 160 ns synthetic deadline still requires a named requester/application. [Microchip 25LC256 DS20005715A, Table 1-2, item 15](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/OTH/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/20005715A.pdf#page=3)

For a binding target decision, allocate two hours to the common CS/SCK/MOSI oracle and time/ownership ledger, four hours to a one-SM PIO attempt and an optimized VM attempt, then two hours to adverse-phase reselect tests and reporting. Use 0.5, 1 and 2 MHz, all 256 opcode values, differently phased clock edges, at least one irregular clock sequence, stopped-SCK abort in an active reply, and a valid different command on immediate reselection. Require correct data, MISO high impedance during command and within 160 ns of the abort, no stale response, 60/40 ns receiver setup/hold and no mid-frame host help. Record queueing policy, actual cold/rearm accesses, high-impedance instrumentation and resident words. A cap that leaves no complete port is an engineering effort observation. It does not exclude other programs. A second SM, programmable event detector, native peripheral or external switch constitutes an additional-cost variant. With 160 ns equaling eight 50 MHz core intervals, even a synchronized path with three input and one output mapping-related intervals leaves at most four whole intervals for detection/decision in the ideal discrete grid. Arrival phase, routing, pad delay and any external switch reduce that budget on a board.

I would give the delivery engineer-day to an executable cold-start host recipe, or an available board and observer measuring owner handoff, if shipping usable artifacts is the decision at hand. The SPI and 1-Wire models cannot tell a first-time user which release to flash, prove peer high impedance, or measure clock continuity. Use a fresh operator under timed conditions and a known independent capture. A software-only pass can justify a documented digital recipe. A physically supported claim needs a measured fixture with shared ownership and a defensible electrical envelope. A same-process area winner becomes actionable only after an actual workload needs it and the whole-system accounting survives this gate.

## Interpreting the chair's burst-removal map

The chair has preregistered a one-line decoded-period ablation using the same whole top, standard-cell Liberty, actual unchanged macro and matched mapping procedure. A lower mapped result would establish an optimization for this realization and nominal mapping. It would not make removal free to existing callers, whose burst opcodes must now fail, or establish that a more aggressive manual-only decoder is minimal. The fallback doubles some UART loader work and changes public success/fault PCs. Constraining a period to zero also permits cross-boundary constant folding, dead state elimination, cell resizing or a changed ABC mapping heuristic. Compare actual sequential-bit removal and the cell-type area breakdown in addition to the scalar result. Divide by both standard-cell area and whole top-plus-macro area. Evaluate the cost against acquired physical margin and the mandatory software contract before allocating a fresh route. A misleading saving can arise if an unchanged fixed macro or tile and its routing floor dominate the fit, or if mutable host compatibility should take precedence under the deadline.

The evidence that would change that vote is a named new hardware constraint that the current chip misses, a reproducible mapped reduction large enough against the total budget to plausibly cross that constraint, a versioned compatibility plan, preservation of the newly chosen workload contracts, and a subsequently acquired routed result including the tight hold/DRC regions. For a question about soft-core resource allocation, a matched logic map can already be valuable even when a competition hardware revision would be premature.

At the chair's 16:02 UTC update the nominal result was available. Logic fell from 44,717.8158 to 42,587.7858 µm², a 2,130.0300 µm² or 4.7633% reduction. Including the unchanged 49,488.4992 µm² macro, the difference is 2.2610% of 94,206.3150 µm². The standard-cell count fell from 3,227 to 2,966 while both used 353 sequential cells. The numerical result therefore supports a combinational/mapping reduction in this implementation. The unchanged sequential-cell count precludes claiming that registers disappeared in number. Cell types may still have changed. The outcome gives the research comparison a measured marginal silicon estimate while leaving current release adoption behind the interface and physical gates.
