# Independent review of research, architecture and mathematics

This panel reviewed checkout `e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e` before reading the other panels' opinions. Three perspectives share one AI reviewer. Their agreement carries the weight of one interpretation. The fresh SPI and UART screens used the public ports of the current RTL, a single local Icarus build, the hash-checked vendor digital SRAM models and H: scratch. The source inspection and arithmetic have different evidentiary roles from those observations.

## Research methodology

The project can defend an existence result. One post-fabrication-loadable core implements the specified UART batches, all four SPI modes in both bit orders, three finite I2C transaction shapes and a triggered stimulus. Original negative results changed decisions. The 8-word timed P design saved 13.1456% routed standard-cell area against compact10 under D34 conditions and failed a fanout limit of eight with eleven loads. Register-backed literal255 failed placement, and even the smaller shared register-store variant failed global routing. Replacing that store with the actual SRAM reduced the matched **mapped** standard-cell-plus-macro footprint from 477,788.2578 to 102,578.2590 µm². Separate G4 routed records reproduce timing and checks. These observations support the storage decision and provide a qualified feasibility point. Global optimality, the area benefit of any proposed software change, average power, measured bit-error rate and silicon timing remain open. [G1 decision](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/evidence/E0031-8x4-physical-comparison/decision.json) · [Architecture and mapped comparison](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/docs/architecture.md) · [G4 reproduction](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/evidence/E0047-g4-submission-candidate/reproduction.json)

H1 and H4 acquire support at the exact mandatory roles, timing points and host assumptions. H2 has case evidence that independently specified traces can be predicted. The project has no matched comparison of verification effort or defect detection against another architecture. A bounded WAIT_PIN also permits a backward timeout branch, so an arbitrary loaded image need not have a finite execution deadline. H3 lacks a current-capacity opcode ablation and a scope-matched competitor. Eight of the thirteen G3 examples are mode/order variants of SPI. G5 contributes one application outside that protocol set. Repeated runs and bit-order variants add coverage to a workload without increasing the number of distinct application classes. [Evaluation contract](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/docs/research/evaluation-contract.md) · [ISA control flow](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/docs/isa.md) · [Program catalog](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/programs/od128-sram-v1/examples.json)

One comparison needs a mathematical correction. Relaxing reprogrammability gives a fixed-function **optimization problem** whose optimum can lower-bound the programmable optimum under identical workload and technology assumptions. Measuring one fixed RTL implementation gives one feasible cost for that relaxed problem. The best unknown fixed design can cost less, and the measured design can cost more than a competitor's programmable implementation. Use a fixed block as an empirical point with its actual features. Assign a lower bound only after a corresponding argument about all admissible designs. [September landscape](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/docs/research/architecture-landscape.md)

**Self-criticism.** The nine mandatory workloads helped shape the ISA. Passing them and one additional waveform gives weak evidence for unanticipated applications. A rich competitor may also offer capabilities these workloads ignore. Choose a held-out application and record host obligations before growing the comparison.

## Computer architecture

The combined I2C example occupies 96 of 128 logical words, the largest in the released catalog. The read and write examples use 53 and 54 respectively. The 256×32 physical macro supplies 8,192 bits for 4,096 exposed instruction bits. The largest image consequently occupies 75% of the logical store and 37.5% of physical bits. Unused cells have no marginal silicon area saving within this fixed macro. The current store uses a 128-bit validity mask, a word-zero bypass and one synchronous port for fetch or loading. Using its upper half for more instructions or a FIFO would require changes to address validity, metadata, loader/fetch timing or arbitration. The current SRAM read enable is tied high and its memory enable follows reset. An activity study could characterize repeated idle reads and identify safe gating conditions. Program-byte readback is legal on disabled edges, so simply qualifying memory access with `ena` would change the interface. [Program store RTL](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/src/m1_program_store.v) · [SRAM integration contract](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/docs/specs/g2-sram-prefetch-screen.md)

The newly available store capacity can address an existing input timing limitation today. The production HELD programs sample internally at nominal SPI edge S+24, consuming the raw MISO sampled at S+22. At the 50 MHz target that raw observation is 440 ns after S. Splitting SAMPLE and WAIT yields a 9- or 11-word image, captures at S+2 and consumes the raw value at S. This alternative already existed in E0027 as a 16-slot instruction-model schedule. We compared all eight binaries to the archived words and loaded them on the actual 128-word design. The change moves receive-valid 22 clocks earlier, adds one to the terminal PC and changes intermediate dispatch/status. The driven pin values, output enables and HALT clocks matched across families. [Historical reference and original hardware boundary](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/evidence/E0027-four-mode-schedule-screen/README.md) · [Published HELD assumptions](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/programs/od128-sram-v1/README.md)

The Icarus pilot judged 112 cases. Eighty-eight EXACT cases passed, eight published HELD cases passed, eight HELD edge-only controls and eight deliberately early-sample controls returned the wrong byte as predicted. All 56,976 public loader/runtime observations were retained. Pushing 0x69 at the mode-0 nominal sample ticks yielded 0x69 at edge 380 with EXACT and 0x96 at edge 402 with HELD. The early control returned 0x96 at edge 379. All three halted on edge 404. A first attempt produced unknown outputs on its first reset edge with a static time-zero driver. A prospectively recorded testbench correction introduced the explicit drive at 1 ns before the first 10 ns rising edge. The original observation remains a failed run. The successful trace checks digital values at sampling ticks. Physical setup/hold around an actual returned SCK edge, board skew, pad timing and metastability remain unmeasured.

**Self-criticism.** One simulator, one toolchain instance, the project's existing oracle, selected payloads and 25-clock half-period leave correlated model errors possible. Commercial SPI devices often hold MISO beyond the sample edge. The old and new programs might both satisfy a particular device. The released HELD examples retain their published contract and will be needed as continuity controls.

## Mathematics

Let P=434 receiver clocks, T be the constant transmitter bit period measured in receiver-clock intervals, and s the actual start time of frame one. Both frames have 8N1 framing and no interframe gap. A raw transition on a sampling edge is visible on that edge in the mathematical input model. The detector consumes it two clocks later. For bit cell k=0 through 9, including start and stop, the raw sample falls at `ceil(s)+(k+1/2)P`. An all-phase check needs `kT <= ceil(s)-s+(k+1/2)P < (k+1)T`. The start detector must re-arm before the second start and remain within its timeout. We checked these conditions in exact rational arithmetic at 98%, 99%, 100%, 101% and 102% of nominal transmitter **baud**. At these rates the back-to-back capture gap is `ceil(s+10T)-ceil(s)`, because each frame starts a new alignment. Its smallest value over all phases is `floor(10T)`.

The E0019 synchronous host polls every H edges and copies L edges after the first positive poll. The largest delay from capture to copy is H−1+L. Copying on the overwrite edge sees the second byte. The all-phase admission rule for this experiment is therefore **H+L ≤ floor(10T)**. At exact rate this gives the documented 4,340 clocks. At +2% it gives 4,254 clocks, or 85.08 µs at the design target. The bound changes before the framing samples fail. In the 20 public Icarus cases, both captured bytes and HALT matched the analysis. The original last-safe exact-rate schedule H=4,338 and L=2 copied 0xAA in place of the first byte 0x55 in each of eight faster-transmitter cases. A host obeying the revised bound copied 0x55 in all twenty. At +1%, phase 0.25, the raw captures occurred at 3,696 and 7,993. With the last-safe exact-rate policy the nominal first copy at 8,035 was already 42 clocks late. The terminal read still returned 0xAA. The existing exact-rate qualification remains valid. [Original RX sampling edges](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/docs/specs/p-uart-rx-8n1.md) · [Host policy and inequality](https://github.com/KG-khangelani/protocol-emulator-asic/blob/e90cca228490423a7fe7c4ae40f7e669200b4d0e/docs/specs/p-uart-rx-host-service.md)

If the requirement becomes recovering two arbitrary ordered bytes with **only terminal reads**, fix the second byte and vary the first over 256 values. The final raw byte and accepted terminal status are identical for all 256 first-byte choices on the published fixed image. Any design that recovers the pair must expose at least eight more bits of distinguishable information somewhere, or let an external observer cache the first byte before overwrite. This lower bound concerns information. It specifies neither FIFO depth for a longer stream nor the transistor cost of implementing validity, framing, overrun and host-side transport.

**Self-criticism.** The UART experiment assumes constant frequency, clean bit transitions, uninterrupted chip enable and a clock-synchronous host. Only one pair of payloads and four start phases were run in RTL. The mathematical all-phase inequality, exercised digital cases and physical receiver tolerance are three different results. A real host's worst scheduling latency and selector settling require measurement. A fixed-frequency margin cannot cover unbounded per-bit jitter.

## Priorities and questions for debate

1. **Qualify an additive SPI firmware profile.** High digital evidence, one extra word, low implementation risk. Allow one to two engineer-days for an independent input model or second trace adapter, both simulators, all modes/orders, byte alphabet and edge-only and held extremes. Require unchanged old binaries and the G3 guard. Check all outputs and timing, changed raw-valid edge and terminal PC, package provenance and one missing-shift negative. A failed boundary or wire trace rejects the proposed profile. Physical device compatibility needs separate pad/board evidence.
2. **Measure an actual UART host and qualify the rate envelope it needs.** The first-copy counterexample makes this worthwhile if the team wants to support independent clocks. Allow one engineer-day for a two-simulator pilot, with rate/phase boundaries, complementary pairs, deliberate stop faults, service equality and equality+1 cases. Use distinct runs for per-bit jitter or a physical fixture. Require correctly validated pairs under the new bound plus a reproducible one-edge counterexample. Measure the longest polling, selector settling and copy intervals for a real adapter before making a hardware timing claim.
3. **Run a scope-matched PIO comparator after submission-critical work.** Use one complete I2C write/repeated-START/read, a NACK and a held-low release as an initial 2–3 engineer-day screen. Freeze initial preload, no further host intervention during that transaction, exact deadline/cleanup, public reload, input latency, clock and pins. Measure resident plus feeder data, host transfers, response bounds and oracle differences. Accept progression to all nine workloads only with all three paths passing and attributable storage. Report a capped failed port as such. Full matched routed implementations and measured effort are prerequisites for architecture superiority.
4. **Test whether SHIFT_BURST still earns its cost at 128 words.** It occurs in both UART images. An isolated firmware-only experiment can unroll the two-frame loop and use manual shifts, per-frame payload selection and counted waits, while preserving both host policies. Budget two working days. Accept a firmware ablation only with identical public timing and payloads at P434 and the P4 corner, frame errors and mandatory regressions. Count added words and changed host assumptions. Archive a failure. A successful firmware test still leaves the area/verification cost of removing hardware to a separate isolated requalification.
5. **Defer added lanes, autonomous FIFO, larger memory and Fmax work without a named unmet workload.** A missed host latency or a requirement for concurrent interfaces would change the priority. Stop representing smaller resident programs or FPGA LUTs as a complete ASIC area comparison. Preserve the G4 physical baseline throughout.

Questions for the other panels: Can a concrete board host meet H and L under independent clocks and give the target shared-pin isolation during loading? What measurement would distinguish useful removal of the SPI 22-clock hold from a target that already meets the old premise? Should the first independent comparator spend scarce effort on the published mandatory host semantics or on an added stream-length requirement, and which decision would each answer change?
