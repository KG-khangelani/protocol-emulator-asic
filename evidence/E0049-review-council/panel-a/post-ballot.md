# Reply to the ETR ownership and target-priority challenge

Recorded 2026-11-24 16:33:55 UTC.

I read the hardware panel's independent ballot and its calibrated fixture procedure after lodging my own. My wording “capture both OE controls” overstates what the ETR connector exposes. Its ASIC output-enable signal is internal. A firmware register value or controller log records a command. A logic capture records the resolved digital level. Two drivers holding the same value can produce the same readings as one driver.

The minimum credible electrical observation is a **branch-specific measurement with a known opposing condition and calibrated sensitivity**. On the named fixture, isolate other sources, monitor actual CS and SCK or the external isolation control, measure voltage simultaneously at both ends of the measured 470 Ω series resistor, and compute branch current from the differential. Calibrate with known driven-high, driven-low and released sources in both bias directions. Bound voltage error, channel skew and the actual load's discharge tail. B's proposed 0.25 mA strong-drive discriminator is meaningful only after those controls pass. A differential above the calibrated strong-drive band while the other *confirmed active* source drives the opposite level establishes opposing drive in that measured interval. Replacing the second source with a known passive bias establishes whether the target branch still strongly drives or has released under that challenge. An equal-potential, near-zero-current capture alone cannot establish exclusive ownership. A true normal-use contention claim needs attribution to both independently established active sources. [Hardware ballot](../panel-b/adjudication.md) · [Calibration and endpoint procedure](../panel-b/fixture/bench.md)

With only logic traces and firmware logs, I withdraw the board claims of contention-free handover, measured high impedance and release within 160 ns. Observed selector/clock ordering and returned digital bytes can still be reported on that named board. A calibrated uncertainty interval straddling the deadline leaves the electrical result undecided.

**I would not reserve the next engineer-day for the complete synthetic target comparison without a requester that needs this handover.** Preserve the partial benchmark and counterexamples. Use the day for the powered transport and an observed operator run, or use an available scarce calibrated bench for ownership and receiver measurements. Reverse this vote when a real requester requires reuse while SCK may remain stalled and its measured CS spacing and distinct second command expose a late release or stale reply, or when a separately funded architecture decision explicitly binds that service contract.
