# Powered launch on the G4 submitted netlist

The chair registered this follow-up after panels B and C independently found that the published macro-pin loader uses a low enable while powered. Both reported a promising two-clock loader hold on production RTL. This screen tests a more consequential claim through the original G3 wire oracles on the actual submitted netlist.

**Hypothesis.** All thirteen G3 images and their 55 acceptance/control cases retain their public results if the selected chip has enable physically tied high, the two idle clocks use the ignored register-write selector `E7` hex, and the host clears bit 7 first (`E7 → 67 → 00`) with clock low before execution. The external target supplies the specified idle levels during the two holds. Test only this operating condition. A real board must also prevent host/target contention and meet settling times.

**Frozen inputs.** Take the submitted Verilog from E0047 candidate run 43543626249. Verify its SHA-256 against the audit and against the reproduction run's identity. Check the two SRAM models and the pinned IHP library Verilog used in the G2 corrected netlist-pair record. Assemble the release programs against the project's frozen independent word vectors. Use the unchanged public G3 UART, SPI and I2C stimuli, response checks, negative controls, trace normalizer and case counts. In an isolated copy change only the fixture root, the loader's physical selector/enable/clock-low handoff, and the testbench connection of the project's enable. The fixture can observe chip-port data and direction. This layer does not implement pad thresholds or a microcontroller driver.

**Oracle and falsifiers.** Require all four test groups to run, with 55 distinct cases and exactly 15 expected rejections. Compare each complete normalized public record, including digest, case disposition, program identity where recorded and observation count, to the Icarus records in E0047's fresh G3 program-tool archive. A changed wire trace, missing case, mismatch in a negative outcome, wrong source/model digest or an arm that cannot preserve the two expected flush observations rejects this screen. The existing wrong-period, data, interface, SPI and I2C controls stay active. The separate panel RTL controls distinguish naive enable substitution from the proposed sequence.

**Budget.** One existing Icarus workbench, up to 40 MiB of H: scratch for the netlist, compiled simulation and records. The primary attempt has a 60-minute simulation cap. Any harness failure is retained before repair. End investigation by 16:40 UTC to leave time for adjudication. A raw success status alone cannot qualify the result. No new synthesis, place-and-route, protocol interface version or public release follows from this screen.
